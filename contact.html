Joining the lab

We welcome inquiries from prospective Ph.D. students, postdocs, and undergraduates interested in physical acoustics, biomedical ultrasound, signal processing, and machine learning for wave-based imaging. Strong candidates have a background in physics, mechanical or biomedical engineering, electrical engineering, or applied math — and a curiosity for messy, complex media.
