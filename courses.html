Courses.

Course materials for current students live on Moodle.

MAE 310 Fall

Heat Transfer Fundamentals

Steady-state and transient one- and multi-dimensional heat conduction using analytical and numerical techniques. Integration of thermodynamics and fluid mechanics into practical convective heat transfer relations relevant to mechanical engineers. Radiation heat transfer.

MAE 308 Spring

Fluid Mechanics

Development of the basic equations of fluid mechanics in general and specialized form. Applications include fluid statics; inviscid, incompressible flow; viscous flow in pipes; the Navier–Stokes equations; and the design of fluid-dynamic systems.

MAE 555 / 495 Spring

Applied Acoustics

Principles of acoustic and elastic wave propagation in fluids and solids. Diffraction theory for finite sources. Wavepackets, dispersion, and waveguiding. Foundations of elasticity and elastic wave propagation in solids via tensor analysis. Time reversal of acoustic waves, with applications to medical imaging, elasticity imaging, and nondestructive testing.

MAE 518 Fall

Acoustic Radiation

Principles of acoustic radiation from vibrating bodies. Radiation of simple sources, propagation in confined spaces, and transmission across different media.

MAE 496 Every semester

Undergraduate Research Projects

Independent research projects in signal processing, experimental acoustics, and the principles of wave propagation. Open to motivated undergraduates — please get in touch.
