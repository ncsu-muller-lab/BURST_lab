  • March 2026
    Award

    Marie Muller honored at the 2026 Engaged University Awards.

    Marie received the 2026 Outstanding Extension and Engagement Award for the College of Engineering and was formally inducted into NC State's Academy of Outstanding Faculty in Extension and Engagement.

    Read the announcement →

  • March 2026
    Fellowship

    Dean's Applied AI Faculty Scholarly Fellowship awarded to the lab.

    Marie received one of the College of Engineering's inaugural Dean's Applied AI Faculty Scholarly Fellowships for “Building AI Expertise to Transform Quantitative Ultrasound and Reveal Hidden Tissue Information.” The project integrates physics-based scattering models with deep learning to extract tissue features from raw ultrasonic waves.

    Read the announcement →

  • 2026
    Publication

    New paper accepted in JASA on cortical bone characterization.

    Austin McCandless, Kay Raum & Marie Muller. Towards Harmonizing Ultrasound-Based Cortical Bone Characterization: A Comparison of Attenuation Measurement Techniques. Journal of the Acoustical Society of America (accepted).

  • 2026
    Event

    UITC Symposium successful conclusion.

    The lab co-organized the Ultrasonic Industrial & Technology Conference (UITC) symposium, hosting researchers across academia and industry to discuss new frontiers in acoustic wave imaging and materials characterization.

  • 2025
    Honor

    Marie Muller named NC State University Faculty Scholar.

    The University Faculty Scholars program honors emerging academic leaders at NC State who demonstrate significant achievement in research, teaching, and scholarship.

  • November 2024
    Grant

    NIH awards $2.65 Million grant for quantitative lung disease research.

    Dr. Muller and collaborator Dr. Jonathan Mamou were awarded a $2.65M grant from the National Institutes of Health (NIH) to advance quantitative ultrasound (QUS) methods for diagnosing and monitoring interstitial lung diseases (ILD), offering a non-invasive, radiation-free alternative to CT scans.

    Read the NC State MAE article →

  • August 2024
    Publication

    Paper on lung quantitative ultrasound published in Scientific Reports.

    Our study, “Lung quantitative ultrasound to stage and monitor interstitial lung diseases,” was published open access in Nature Scientific Reports. Co-lead authors are lab Ph.D. graduates Dr. Azadeh Dashti and Dr. Roshan Roshankhah.

    Read the research news release →

  • December 2023
    Defense

    Congratulations to Dr. Azadeh Dashti!

    Azadeh Dashti successfully defended her Ph.D. dissertation on quantitative lung ultrasound and multiple scattering characterization. Congratulations, Dr. Dashti!

  • October 2023
    Grant

    $2.9 Million NIH grant awarded to assess tumor angiogenesis.

    Dr. Muller is PI on a major NIH award titled “Quantitative Assessment of Angiogenesis using Ultrasound Multiple Scattering.” The research aims to evaluate microvascular architecture non-invasively to identify biomarkers of cancer aggressiveness and plaque rupture.

    Read the announcement →

  • December 2022
    Defense

    Congratulations to Dr. Roshan Roshankhah!

    Roshan Roshankhah successfully defended his Ph.D. dissertation on ultrasound wave propagation and attenuation in complex media. He has joined UNC Chapel Hill as a postdoctoral fellow.
